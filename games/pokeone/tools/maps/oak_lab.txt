[script lab_enter]
if met_oak > end
set met_oak
face oak down
say@Prof._Oak Ah, {player}! There you are.
say@Prof._Oak I've been waiting. Your very own Pokémon journey starts today!
questdone the_pokemon_prof
say@Prof._Oak There are three Poké Balls on that table.\nGo on, choose one!
say@Gary Hey, Gramps! What about me?
say@Prof._Oak Be patient, {rival}. You can have one, too.

[script oak]
if starter_chosen > after
say Go ahead, {player}. Choose one of the Pokémon on the table!
end
:after
if got_pokedex > advice
set got_pokedex
say {player}, take this with you. It's the Pokédex, a hi-tech encyclopedia!
sfx item
say {player} received the Pokédex!
say It automatically records data on Pokémon you've seen or caught.
:advice
say {player}, Route 1 is north of Pallet Town.\nA young trainer named Joey is waiting there.
say Why not challenge him and see what your Pokémon can do?

[script gary]

say Heh, I don't need to be greedy like you.\nGo ahead and choose, {player}!
end
:after
say My Pokémon looks a lot tougher than yours.

[script dex_case]
