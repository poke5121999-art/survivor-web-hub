   (V4Van ~1:00; prompts "Should I choose this? [Space]" / "Let's look at the other pockets too [Esc]").
2. Prologue storybook art + night cutscene with a lilac-haired child (Van = the default player name in V4Van, name is typed by player)
   and a Balrog attack on the hero's slime; wakes in the Larus inn run by Amy. [SEEN] V4Van 0:20-4:30
   frames\v4van_0020_prologue_art.jpeg, v4van_dialogue_amy_inn.jpeg. Story: you are a castaway/amnesiac, want to return to your hometown.
   Allen (Larus Storage clerk) "found a lead on how you can return to your hometown" [SEEN sheet3]. "Village of Rough Waves" is Larus's
   subtitle (title card, Goget 1:30). [SEEN]
3. Allen: quest "The Herb in the Box" (find herb in boxes; "some boxes won't have a Herb") [SEEN sheet1]. Slime list tutorial: Allen explains
   Larus Storage shows tamed slimes [SEEN V4Van ~7:00-9:00].
4. Lucy (artist NPC at the fountain) asks for a Potion, then sends you to Shadow Forest Entrance: "Grant Lucy's Request: Defeat Junior
   Floralime 2/2, Small Slime Fragment 0/1". [SEEN frames\v4van_hud_shadow_forest_quest.jpeg, V4Van ~14:00]
5. Rudga (guide) teaches "Combat Controls & Whistle": attack with partner, press whistle to stop the battle and recall the slime. [SEEN sheet1]
   Then a tutorial PvP duel in a fenced sand arena vs Rudga (1v1, Team A/B, 5:00 timer). [SEEN frames\goget_pvp_arena_team_ab.jpeg, Goget ~3:30]
6. "Potential" tutorial (Allen): you gain a Potential point each level-up; spend (SP) in a Potential tree to unlock skills/traits.

   (Cube = capture item) [SEEN frames\v4van_sheet_friendly_slime_shop.jpg; Goget 6:00-8:00].

   Researcher quest: tame Junior Floralime 0/1 ("Tame Junior Floralime") [SEEN sheet2 last tile].
9. Capture: weaken wild slime in real-time battle, then throw a Cube at it with E; the wild slime's tag becomes "Untamed" -> "Tame".
   It may break free ("nakatakas", retry) [SAID GM WALL 6:56; SEEN frames\gmwall_tame_floralime_cube.jpeg, v4van_capture_untamed_prince.jpeg].
   Success -> "Would you like to give the Red Mandrake a nickname? [Next time][Nickname]" in a lab-capsule scene

    "Caring for a Slime: Small Slime Fragment 19/20, Tweety's Hide 0/3" [SEEN frames\v4van_hud_... sheet3/4]. Synthesis UI itself NOT seen in any clip. [GAP]
11. Slimepedia (collection point total, 001-0xx cards, element icons, filter) with "Discover a new slime" quest, N badges. [SEEN frames\gmwall_slimepedia.jpeg]
12. Later zones for L10+ players: Shadow Forest (Entrance, Misty Hill, Cool Pond, Tainted Wellspring, Forgotten Clearing), Hidden Shore

## 2. Controls and camera (PC)
- Camera: fixed high-angle 3/4 top-down, 3D rendered with chibi models; follows the player; indoor interiors cut away walls. [SEEN everywhere]
  Camera pitch about 55-60 degrees, no visible rotation, no zoom seen. [GUESS]
- Movement: mouse cursor shown on screen; a thin blue line from the player to the cursor in battle/capture frames marks aim direction.
  [SEEN frames\gmwall_tame_floralime_cube.jpeg] WASD movement [GUESS]; Space = confirm in dialogue, Esc = other choice [SEEN prompt glyphs].
- HUD key hints [SEEN frames\gmwall_hud_party_bar.jpeg]: Q, E, R item slots (Q potion, E cube (22 left), R empty/other), a Shift-labeled
  dash/sprint-like icon left of Q (label unreadable "SHIFT" [GUESS]), big circular attack button bottom right with a key glyph under it
  (sword icon; becomes a red whistle icon while a battle is on). Tab = minimap/world map toggle (label "Tab" under minimap). C = key
  next to a ghost/skull icon in boss fight (GM WALL ~4:05 = "C" [SEEN]).
- Chat bubble bottom-left (chat), backpack, mail, quest-log icons top-right, "CH 01" channel label (server channels; CH03, CH36 seen). [SEEN]

## 3. Battle system
- Real-time, no scene change: walk into aggro range of a wild slime and the fight starts on the field. [SAID GM WALL 3:24-4:06]
- Your partner slime fights automatically (follows, attacks); you steer position/aim and can recall it with the whistle button; you can switch which
  slime is out with the slot bar (cooldown/countdown on switch). [SAID GM WALL 3:35; SEEN party bar with up to 5 icons, frames\gmwall_hud_party_bar.jpeg]
- Player is not a fighter: the player avatar has no HP bar in field fights; the slime has HP/MP/stamina style 3 bars (green/blue/yellow) over its head;
  enemies show name, Lv, HP "21/21", "Untamed/Tame" tag. [SEEN frames\v4van_capture_untamed_prince.jpeg]
- Player stamina warning "Low stamina" top-left [SEEN]; deaths happen ("died several times in strong spots") [SAID GM WALL 4:31].
- Party size: 4 slots in early demo (a 5th at Lv 16+ in GM WALL boss frame shows 5 icons). [SEEN]
- Slime detail (Slime Storage panel): name, Lv, element chip (e.g. Grass), EXP, HP, MP, Stamina bars, 4 locked skill slots, Take It button, Bag 10/16.
  [SEEN frames\v4van_sheet_slime_storage.jpg]
- Skills: bundles show Skill_Melee_* and Skill_Range_* clips; boss frame shows 4 skill buttons (Shift, Q, E, R) [SEEN GM WALL boss]. Unlocked by Potential.
- Elements: Grass icon seen (Junior Floralime, Mandrake); Slimepedia entries carry 1-2 element chips (fire, dark seen as icons). Type chart NOT visible
  anywhere in clips. [GAP] Names of 14 elements: from bundles per BRIEF. Damage numbers pop up in red ("2", "16") [SEEN].
- Capture: Cube item (E), requires weakened target, may fail and need retry; better cubes exist ("great ball / master ball" analogue) [SAID GM WALL 7:16].
- PvP: from right-clicking/clicking another player: Add Friend / Invite Party / PVP / Report / Block. [SEEN frames\gmwall_player_menu_pvp.jpeg]
  Arena has Team A / Team B, "1/1" slimes, countdown timer. 1v1 tutorial vs Rudga. [SEEN]
- Party play: co-op boss fights with many players on the same field. [SEEN GM WALL boss frame, chat in Thai]

## 5. Demo scope
- Zones: Larus Village ("Village of Rough Waves"; interiors Inn (Amy), Larus Storage (Allen), Bianca's office/ship cabin, Synthesis lab (Levi),
  fountain square, Vivian's potion/cube shop, Olivia's cart, Lucy at fountain, tavern with beer-mugs sign) -> Shadow Forest (Entrance, Misty Hill,
  Cool Pond, Tainted Wellspring, Forgotten Clearing) -> Hidden Shore (Hidden Coast Passage). [SEEN]
  Maps are gated by quests [SAID GM WALL 4:36]. Channels CH01..CH36+. Noecheon Village added in Oct update. [PAGE]
- Slimes seen: Junior Floralime (Grass, #002), Giant Floralime (#015), Tweety (#016), Tricky (#017), Prince (poison/water?), Mandrake/Red Mandrake,
  Kobold, Poison Lizard, Poison Prince, Gangster (dark), Roasted (red rooster-like fire), Mercury (grey puddle), Twinky (white bunny), Balrog (boss in story).
- NPC names seen: Amy, Allen, Rudga, Lucy, Vivian, Olivia, Billy (village guardian), Bianca, Levi, Martin, Benjamin, Mysterious Researcher.
  Bundle names noecheon / talpha / larus / hydrus / cochlea: only Larus confirmed here. noecheon = Oct update village. Others NOT seen. [GAP]
- Length: GM WALL played "a few hours" and reached only Lv5 at first [SAID 2:44]; the clips show level 16-19 players in boss area. Early demo ~1-2 h; [GUESS]
- Players avatar sprite: chibi, lilac hair default. Art style: bright 3D chibi, UI soft pastel blue, slime-head loading icon.
